## Background

The release delivers the design of 2026-0022: sources, stages, gathers
and sinks, the lifecycle, the role methods, the words and the
operators. Most of it is new since v0.2.0, and DBIx-Squirrel, on its
`pipeline-experimental`, is so far its only user.

## Decisions

- [x] **D1.** Is this release v0.3.0, or v1.0.0? **v0.3.0,** Iain
  decided on 2026-11-13, letting it settle before switching to v1.0.0
  when the time is right. The design is settled, but most of what it
  delivers is unreleased, two parts of it changed on the day this was
  decided (2026-0026 and 2026-0027), and only DBIx-Squirrel has used
  it. Under 1.0, a change to what CLAUDE.md counts as behaviour, the
  messages among it, would be a v2.0.0, and one such change was
  already proposed: a message from DBIx-Squirrel of 2026-11-13 (its
  commit a3708534c87955d69d67887256503ad951957bbb), that `pipeline_in`
  give a named pipeline itself when given one name, which changes how
  stages are numbered in errors. Considered on the way to 1.0: settle
  what could break first, such as that suggestion, with a cold review
  of every public name and default; say what the 1.0 promise covers,
  perhaps leaving messages' wording free to change in minor releases;
  and ship a trial release DBIx-Squirrel depends on from CPAN, then
  v1.0.0 with no changes once the trial has run for a while.
- [x] **D2.** Do `docs/design/` and IMPLEMENTATION.md stay once v0.3.0
  is delivered, or go, their history kept in git and this item's
  record? **They stay on `pipeline-experimental`, and don't come to
  `develop`,** Iain decided on 2026-11-13: they're long, and reading
  them costs a session more than they now give. So the merge's last
  commit on `pipeline-experimental` removes `docs/design/`, which keeps
  them in the history, and the records that link to them say where
  they were. Until then, they aren't kept up to date with the code.
- [x] **D3.** Who drafts the README, rewritten for the stream concept,
  as the design's 20.1 has it: Iain, or Claude? **Claude,** as Iain
  asked on 2026-11-13; it was rewritten that day, in
  3b6804ef19eeda7702723a260f70ba68192b9d34, with every example run.
- [x] **D4.** How does `pipeline-experimental` reach `develop`, without
  `docs/design/`? **By a fast-forward, after one commit that deletes
  `docs/design/`,** Iain decided on 2026-11-13. `develop` has nothing
  of its own since the branch left it, so there's no merge commit.
  History isn't rewritten to take the folder out: 76 commits touch it,
  so every later hash would change, and DBIx-Squirrel's records and
  messages cite Ekorn's hashes. Two signed tags go first:
  `rollback/develop-2026-11-13`, on `develop` as it was
  (124a9a47e8ff8b573343cc27f429308e32920854), to reset it to if need be;
  and `archive/pipeline-experimental`, on the branch's last commit
  with `docs/design/`, which keeps the design reachable by name once
  the branch is gone. The branch is deleted only on Iain's word. Ekorn
  goes first; DBIx-Squirrel's `pipeline-experimental` merges into its
  `develop` after, as Iain will ask its session to.
- [x] **D5.** Does DBIx-Squirrel need v0.3.0 on CPAN, perhaps as a
  TRIAL release, before it merges? DBIx-Squirrel's session suggested
  so. **No,** Iain decided on 2026-11-13: it tests against Ekorn's
  working copy, with `../Ekorn-Pipeline/lib` on its path, and merges
  when satisfied, which also spares a TRIAL that `minil dist` can't
  make. Its cpanfile range moves to `>= v0.3.0, < v0.4.0` once v0.3.0
  is on CPAN. Its CI, if it wants v0.3.0 before then, can install Ekorn
  from GitHub's `develop`. Both decisions went to it as messages,
  DBIx-Squirrel 1526a44 and 0ca3960 on its `pipeline-experimental`.

## The plan

1. Settle D3 to D5, and move the item to Now.
2. Tag `develop` as `rollback/develop-2026-11-13`, and the branch's

Done when v0.3.0 is on CPAN, and DBIx-Squirrel has its task.
