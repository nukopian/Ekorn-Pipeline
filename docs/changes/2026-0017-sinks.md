## Background

The design's 8.3 lists the sinks in Ekorn::Pipeline::Sinks, none
exported by default: those named for what they give, the `keep`
family and `count_items`, and the containers, `to_fh` and `to_file`,
each the counterpart of a `from_` source. The prototype built all but
`to_fh`. Its POD is a placeholder.

## Decisions

None of its own. A detail the design leaves, taken as `to_file` and
`from_fh` imply: `to_fh` writes as `to_file` does, each item followed
by `$/`, and croaks "Expected an open filehandle" for a handle that
isn't, as `from_fh` does.

## The plan

As the roadmap gave it on 2026-11-13:

> Ekorn::Pipeline::Sinks: the `keep` family (`keep_all`, `keep($n)`,
> `keep_first`, `keep_last`, `keep_single`), `count_items`, and the
> containers `to_fh` and `to_file`, which takes I/O layers, as the
> design's 8.3 lists them; those that can stop the pull early. Built so
> far: all but `to_fh`. Done when each is in, with its POD, tests and
> docs, and coverage above the floors.

Steps: `to_fh`; tests for each sink; the module's POD in full; the
reference's Sinks section, from the draft; Changes.

## What happened

**Shipped, 2026-11-13,** on `pipeline-experimental`, for v0.3.0
(2026-0025), as planned. Against Done when: each sink is in, with its
POD, tests and docs, and Sinks is covered in full. With it, branch
coverage of `lib/` reaches its floor; only POD falls short, in
Operators, 2026-0018's.

- `to_fh` is built; it and `to_file` share one writer, so both add `$/`
  alike, and `to_fh` checks its handle is open, naming "the
  filehandle" where `to_file` names its path in "Can't write %s: %s".
  Sinks exports `:all`.
- `t/19-sinks.t` tests each sink. `to_file` failing to close, as on a
  full disk, is tested by a `close` that fails when asked, given in
  place of Perl's before Sinks loads, as macOS has no `/dev/full`.
- The module's POD is written in full. The reference gains Sinks, from
  the draft without its `|`, which is 2026-0018's, with the exports,
  the modules and the diagnostics. The how-to gains a part, "Getting a
  result from many values", with the draft's four recipes, and
  2026-0014's recipe for a sink of your own moves into it. Each example
  was run.
