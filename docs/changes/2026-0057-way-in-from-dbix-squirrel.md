## Background

DBIx-Squirrel's README, user guide and reference now come in two parts
(its roadmap 2026-0052 to 2026-0058). Part 1 is DBIx::Squirrel alone:
placeholders, the iterator and stages. Part 2, "With Ekorn::Pipeline",
is data crossing the database's edge: query results to files, JSON and
CSV, loads in batches, and tees. It opens with why the two split, and
sends its readers to Ekorn's README, user guide and reference for the
rest.

So readers come to Ekorn's documents from DBIx-Squirrel's knowing
stages, an iterator's methods and a sink given last to a call, under
DBIx::Squirrel's names, but not Ekorn's sources, feeds or words.
DBIx-Squirrel suggests a short way in for them: what they know already,
under which of Ekorn's names, and where to start. It needs nothing:
its links work as they are.

Ekorn isn't tied to the DBI, so its documents are written for anyone
with values to hand out. A way in for one neighbour's readers mustn't
make the rest read as if Ekorn were part of DBIx::Squirrel.

  the rest, as Iain decided on 2026-11-26, as recommended.

## The plan

**What:** a section of the how-to, "Coming from DBIx::Squirrel", after
"Import everything from one place" and before chapter 1, listed in its
Contents, with a line pointing to it from the README's Documentation
(D1); saying what a DBIx::Squirrel reader knows already, under which of
Ekorn's names, what's new to them, and where to start, kept to a
screen (D2).

**Why:** DBIx-Squirrel's Part 2 sends its readers to Ekorn's documents,
knowing stages, an iterator and sinks under DBIx::Squirrel's names
(Background).

**Steps:**

1. `docs/HOWTO.md`: the section, opening with who it's for, so other
   readers can skip it; a table of what they know, each with Ekorn's
   name and the chapter that has it: a statement's `iterate` and an
   iterator, `next` included; stages, `Stop`, `gather` and the canned
   stages, the same subs; `result_number` and `result_original`;
   Tooling; sinks, sources, feeds, `tee`, `|` and names, from Part 2.
   What has no counterpart: `reset`, `pluck` and `as_hash`. Then what's
   new to them, and where to start. One example, a `while` loop over
   an iterator with `next`, checked by `xt/howto.t`.
2. The Contents line for it.
3. `README.pod`: a sentence in Documentation pointing to it;
   `README.md` regenerated by Minilla, as MAINTAINING says.
4. `scripts/manuals` run; `xt/manuals.t`, `xt/howto.t` and
   `xt/spelling.t` passing.
5. `Changes`, under Unreleased: the precis naming roadmap 2026-0057,
   and the entry; the item leaves `ROADMAP.md` in the same commit.
6. A message for DBIx-Squirrel with the section's heading and anchor,
   once it's committed, should it want to link to it.

**Done when:** the section, its Contents line and the README's line
are in; its example runs as its comments say; `yath test` passes;
`Changes` says so; and DBIx-Squirrel has the message.

## What happened

- **Outcome:** as planned. The section is in `docs/HOWTO.md`, after
  "Import everything from one place", with its Contents line: a table
  of six things a DBIx::Squirrel reader knows, each with Ekorn's name
  and chapter; what has no counterpart; one example, a `while` loop
  with `next`, which `xt/howto.t` runs, and which fails when its
  output is changed; then what's new and where to start. The README's
  Documentation points to it. `yath test` passes.
- **Changes from the plan:** the example says `next` is from v0.4.3,
  the next release, and how to call the iterator before it, as readers
  of the how-to on GitHub may have v0.4.2.
- **What's left:** the message for DBIx-Squirrel (step 6), sent after
  this commit.
